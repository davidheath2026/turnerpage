Role, decoded

Not the person building the load. The person who owns whether it should run.

A Data Migration Lead is a governance and coordination role, not the hands-on technical build — that craft belongs to the Technical Architect and Data Engineer. Your job is making sure the right evidence exists, the right people are aligned, and the programme knows the true state of readiness at every stage.

It's a role built on judgement more than throughput. You're the conduit between the technical delivery team and the business or client stakeholders — translating "the load failed on the vendor table" into what it actually means for go-live, and translating "we need this by Friday" back into what the team can realistically deliver.

Your time on a project flexes with how self-sufficient the client's own team is — heavier during onboarding and framework setup, lighter once knowledge transfer sticks. But it never disappears entirely. Someone still has to own the risk review and the escalation when it actually matters, and that's the seat this course trains you for.

A Data Migration Lead, day to day
  • Confirms environment and configuration/reference data are actually ready before migration activities start
  • Works daily alongside the Project Manager, Solution Architect, and Technical Architect
  • Identifies blockers early and triages bugs before they threaten the timeline
  • Is the conduit between the technical delivery team and the business or client stakeholders
  • Owns the readiness recommendation — proceed, delay, or escalate — and defends it to governance
