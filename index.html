What makes this different

Professional judgement, not passive content

Turner Page combines realistic ERP scenarios, practical frameworks, coached decisions and reflection. The aim is not simply to teach what Data Migration Leads do. It is to develop how they think.

1

Face a real project decision

Begin with the kind of question Data Migration Leads are expected to answer when evidence is incomplete and pressure is rising.

2

Learn a practical framework

Use the Turner Page Migration Readiness Framework to assess data, process, business, environment and residual risk.

3

Apply and reflect

Test your reasoning, receive coaching and identify the idea you will take into your next project.
